Data Product Management

Data Product Management

Software product management has been growing for nearly 30 years, but the challenges posed by data (and now AI) require a more bespoke approach. We can empower your data people to become product managers, or help existing PMs grasp the value data brings to their software or AI products. Whether you need help hiring, training, or embedding an expert to deliver a new product — we can help.

Data Product Strategy

Delivering AI or software products with a strong data core requires you to think beyond user experience and product design — you also need a data governance model that keeps data accurate and secure while opening it up for your business needs. We align your product strategy with the data foundations you need, using the Archernar Strategy System.

Data Product Roadmap

Once you have the people and strategy, you need to set to work. Using the Archernar Strategy System, we translate your strategic goals into a deliverable, measurable roadmap of outcomes.

Commercialising Data

Whether you want to commercialise data through new software products, sell it directly, or use it to inform decisions — treating data like any other IP or physical asset is a great place to start.

Data Asset Audit

Before investing in a Data Catalog and the supporting processes and people, capture and understand your core datasets to identify and prioritise urgent actions.

Data Value Prioritisation

Identify your top commercial opportunities to make better use of data — whether market-facing, cost-saving or risk-related. Work with key colleagues to set a pragmatic path forward.

Market Scan

What are your competitors, partners or peers working on that could offer inspiration, opportunity or risk to your data approach? We help you read the landscape clearly.

Data as a Product

Bring Product Thinking to your approach to data. Create internal or external data products developed and managed sustainably. Build and empower your data product team to deliver measurable value.

04

Data Sharing & Economic Growth

Data is a team sport. Working with others can multiply the benefits to companies, charities and governments. Entire societies can benefit if data sharing is handled in the right way.

Open Data

Government entities are often required to release open data by law. With limited resource, this can be challenging and the ROI poorly realised. We help identify the most valuable data to release and understand users better — and help businesses understand the value open data can bring as both user and publisher.

Data Sharing Programmes

Not all data can be open. Not all data can be sold. Some can be shared in a controlled way that benefits both publisher and user while maintaining privacy and security — internally, across defined partners, or with academic/public sector organisations. Creating those mechanisms requires strong data and product management. Archernar can help.

Data Literacy

The ability to read, work with, analyse and communicate with data is the key skill of the 21st century. Not everyone needs to be a data scientist. We help your people take practical steps to becoming data-enabled, and help your data people boost their business literacy so their great work isn't misunderstood.

05

Ethical & Compliant Data Use

Regulations like GDPR changed how data risks are understood. But too many leadership teams only see the risk — missing opportunities to lead the way in ethical data use. With AI regulations incoming globally, an early ethical approach reduces risk later.

AI Literacy

Get the basics without the hype. What can AI do to transform your business? What are the risks? And more importantly — what does your data strategy need to deliver to enable ethical, secure AI?

Data Ethics Panel

Embed ethics in all data decisions to move beyond box-ticking compliance toward a state where your business can hold itself up as an exemplar to customers, investors and regulators.

DPO-as-a-Service

If a full-time Data Protection Officer isn't yet a necessity, access fractional DPO services to support key data decisions. Our consultants are CIPPe certified to help you navigate GDPR and similar privacy regulations while supporting your commercial data aims.
